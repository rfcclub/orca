# Decision record

Issue:

## Question

State the question, scope, environment and constraints.

## Options

List viable alternatives and distinguishing measurements. A spike states its
question, budget and disposal/retention policy. Retained production code follows TDD.

## Decision

Record the choice and rationale. Link human acceptance for the exact outcome
contract digest; approval of the original intent does not accept this decision.

## Consequences

Record costs, unknowns and conditions for revisiting the choice.

## Obligations

Record each obligation's verification method and either captured verification
evidence or a linked deferred follow-up. Acceptance does not mean implementation.
